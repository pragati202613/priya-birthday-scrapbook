A Little Letter for You

Dear Priya,

Happy Birthday! ❤️

It's funny how a simple test in 8th class brought us together. Who would've thought that sitting beside each other would turn into four years of memories, endless conversations, calls, chats, laughter and so many silly moments?

I still remember how we could talk for hours about absolutely anything and everything. You were always someone who listened to me, understood me, motivated me and somehow made even the most ordinary days fun.

I know things aren't quite the same as they used to be. We don't talk as much as before, and a lot has changed over these last two years. But that doesn't mean the memories have become any less special to me. I genuinely miss the way we used to be, our random conversations and all those little moments that made our friendship so special.

I don't know what the future holds, but I really hope that someday we find our way back to that old closeness. No pressure, no expectations — just a little hope.

And please, never stop being your funny, understanding and beautiful self. Also, I hope we never run out of people to gossip about because otherwise, what will happen to our Bhindi and Tejpata department? 😂

I wish you endless happiness, success, love and all the beautiful things you deserve.

Thank you for being such a special part of my life. No matter how much things change, I'll always be grateful that one random school test brought us together.

Happy Birthday once again! Love you! ❤️

From,
Your partner in unexpected friendship
